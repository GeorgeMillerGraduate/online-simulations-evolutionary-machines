What is being learned?
In neural mode, 24 normalised physical observations enter a small tanh network. Its outputs set bounded motor speeds or cart force. Network inference runs at 20 Hz and physics at 60 Hz. Weights evolve across generations; they do not learn within a lifetime in Build 1.
The original car, walker, jumper and balancer controllers remain available as legacy baselines. Body topology is fixed. Optional body evolution changes dimensions and physical parameters, not the number of limbs. Decorative brass heads and arm casings are not extra physical actuators.
The large arena replays the latest generation’s training winner on its first training course at 1×. Its displayed fitness is one replay score; selection uses the mean over all training courses. Research mode skips presentation waits. Validation and final-test results never enter parent selection.
Genetic diversity and behavioural diversity are different quantities. The behavioural plot uses measured progress, upright fraction, contact fraction and action variation. It is a defined descriptor, not proof of open-ended intelligence.
Course intervals describe variability across courses. Only the independent-runs study estimates variation across separately seeded evolutionary runs. These tools support experiments; they do not establish a novel research result by themselves.
